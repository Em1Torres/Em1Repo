One of the first issues when it comes to software products is:

- Accessibility: designing products that everyone can use, despite having physical limitations like color blind and stuff

This issue leads us to

- Web accessibility: that every persona can utilize several web applications, and its important for many types of people like older people, disabilities or people from rural areas. This cab be determined with user experience improvement and satisfaction.

**Principles of web accessibility**

- Perceivable: que la información se pueda observar correctamemente.
- Operable: cuestiones de si se puede ocupar con el teclado o con el mouse.
- Understandable: cualquier persona sea capaz de entender la aplicación y su funcionamiento.
- Robust: no importa que pase el tiempo que se pueda seguir utilizando.
